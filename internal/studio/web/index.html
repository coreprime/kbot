Open Unit

Pick a TA unit to view its 3D geometry, drive its COB animations and fire its weapons. Orphan 3DOs (props / features) without an FBI definition also appear at the bottom of the list. The coloured chips on each row show which of the FBI / 3DO / COB files for that unit were found in the loaded VFS.
